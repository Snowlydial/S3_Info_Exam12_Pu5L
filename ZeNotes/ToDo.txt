Puissance 5, turn-based game
=> Players have to align 5 points to win
=> Players can align points horizontally, vertically or diagonally
=> Players can only place one point at a time

Gimmick:
Buttons that suggest for each player (takes the player turn if used)
=> switch btw two modes
	- Strat mode: suggest the best move and only works if YOU have 3 points or more aligned
	- Defensive mode: if the opponent has 4 points aligned, suggest the best move to block him (suggest even if it is checkmate for you in their next move)
		=> Prioritize defense to strat mode (EXCEPTION: YOU have 4 points aligned and it is your turn, then suggest the best move to win)
